Vocabulary words for IELTS: Family
Here are descriptions and examples of the vocabulary words related to the topic of family for your reference to score well in your IELTS test:

Family ties | Upbringing | Siblings | Inheritance | Foster Family

1. Family ties

The emotional connections and relationships among family members.

Example:Despite living far apart, our strong family ties keep us connected and supportive of each other.

2. Upbringing

The way in which parents or guardians raise and educate their children.

Example:Her parents provided her with a strict but loving upbringing that shaped her into a responsible adult.

3. Siblings

Brothers and sisters within the same family.

Example:Sarah is the youngest of three siblings, with an older brother and sister.

4. Inheritance

The assets, property, or money that is passed on to family members after someone's death.

Example:The inheritance from her grandparents allowed her to fulfill her dream of starting a small business.

5. Foster family

A family that provides temporary care and support for children who cannot live with their biological parents.

Example: The foster family gave the child a safe and nurturing environment while her parents addressed their challenges.

Vocabulary words for IELTS: Immediate family
Parentage | Paternal | Maternal | Offspring | Lineage

1. Parentage

The identity of one's parents; the fact of being someone's child.

Example:Her parentage is a blend of different cultures, which enriches her perspective on life.

2. Paternal

Relating to or characteristic of a father.

Example:Her paternal grandfather was a skilled musician, and she inherited his love for playing the piano.

3. Maternal

Relating to or characteristic of a mother.

Example:The maternal instinct kicked in when she saw the injured kitten on the street, prompting her to take care of it.

4. Offspring

Children or young of a particular parent or family.

Example: The swan's protective behaviour towards its offspring was heartwarming to witness.

5. Lineage

The sequence of family members from one generation to another is often traced through the male or female line.

Example: The royal family's lineage can be traced back hundreds of years through historical records.

Vocabulary words for IELTS: Less common family words
Matriarch | Patriarch | Stepchild | Godparent | Half-sibling

1. Matriarch

A female head of a family or community.

Example:The matriarch of the family was respected for her wisdom and leadership.

2. Patriarch

A male head of a family or community.

Example:The patriarch of the village was known for his fair decisions and dedication to his people.

3. Stepchild

A child of one's spouse or partner from a previous marriage or relationship.

Example:Building a strong bond with her stepchild took time and effort, but their relationship is now close and loving.

4. Godparent

A person who sponsors a child's baptism and takes on the role of providing spiritual guidance and support.

Example:The godparents were present during the child's confirmation ceremony, symbolising their ongoing role in her life.

5. Half-sibling

A sibling who shares only one biological parent with you.

Example:Although they have different mothers, they share the same father and are therefore considered half-siblings.

Note: Remember to practice using these vocabulary words in your Writing and Speaking tasks to improve your language proficiency and effectively convey your ideas on the IELTS exam.
